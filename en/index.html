Sand Mandala

Play · listen · breathe · sleep

Strike a Tibetan singing bowl and listen as its sound blooms and slowly fades. Above it, sand trembles and draws a different figure for every note: the real physics of sound, made visible.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPad

Play

Strike the bowls softly or firmly, with felt, leather or wood, or let one sing on its own as the mallet circles its rim. Record what you play.

Listen

Melodies written for the bowls: listen, learn them by following the sand, or play along. And See the Sound, to look inside it.

Breathe and sleep

Night scenes that answer your bowls: the sea, rain, a pond, a fairy forest, whales… And they breathe with you, six times a minute.

Wake

Alarms with singing-bowl melodies and a sunrise that comes on gently. With Siri, Shortcuts and widgets.
